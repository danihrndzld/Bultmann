<|det|>text [118, 308, 951, 759]<|/det|>This is the final service of the term. It is an hour of farewell, in which we bid goodbye to the term and all that it has brought us in the way of tasks completed. I hope also that it has enriched us with new joy, new impressions and new insights, with knowledge that has been inspiring and also perhaps a little frightening; in any event I hope that we have been both stirred and encouraged. For many also it is an hour of farewell to the place in which we have worked together, to the town of Marburg and its surrounding country. And the more our life is consciously or unconsciously moulded and coloured by the character of the environment in which we move, so much the more does our departure from it strike for us a solemn and significant note. Above all it is a time in which we bid farewell to each other, we who have been linked together as learners and teachers; and while for some it means only a temporary departure which will be followed by another term of work together, for many too it is a final farewell or at any rate a farewell for some considerable time, broken at most by occasional meetings later on.
