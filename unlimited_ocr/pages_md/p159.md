<|det|>text [65, 104, 890, 445]<|/det|>For why is it that the mighty creative and guiding power of God remains hidden from us? Why is it that we so seldom, or not at all, experience the wondrous things God can accomplish for us? It is simply because we wish to, and suppose that we can, control our lives by our own initiative; it is because we wish to live in our own strength, by our own will, by the ingenuity of our own planning. Accordingly we accept as real and valid that view of the world which fits into this outlook. This view however is one in which the world and all that happens within it appears to be controlled by law, the sequence of cause and effect; it is the world whose energies we master for our own purposes; the world which we shape according to our own designs. And so long as we see the world from this angle, it will not disclose to us the mighty power, the guidance and government of God.
<|det|>text [60, 446, 885, 897]<|/det|>Of course there is no question that the world may be understood and controlled in this way in so far as it is the world of our daily work.
