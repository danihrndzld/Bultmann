<|det|>text [120, 34, 949, 486]<|/det|>Yes, this world becomes God's garden, its apparent reality becomes genuine reality as a result of the fact that we are invited to encounter therein the love of God and are privileged to impart that love to others. And if our joy this Christmastide is to be and must be that we receive anew the gift of divine love and are freed by it to love with new power and inspiration, is not that something which will unite us with those who are fighting for us? They too are offered the gift of this joy and upon them too has this light shined, and all of us, both those at home and those out there at the front, must help each other to prepare ourselves to receive this joy and this light. At Advent we should make it clear to ourselves that we are faced here by an issue which calls for vital decision. Do we or do we not desire these divine gifts? Do we truly want the heavenly light to shine in our earthly darkness? Are we prepared to admit that our world, and that we ourselves, are wrapped in thick darkness? Let us rightly understand this summons to decision, let us obey the call to discipleship! Which of us can tell how much longer he will have to hear the Word?
