<|det|>text [129, 75, 955, 368]<|/det|>Such are the fruits of impatience, of the incapacity to bear our burdens and to wait, to lend a hand patiently so that in time the evil may diminish. Impatience leads only to a worsening of our troubles. It poisons the atmosphere. And what we need above all is an atmosphere that is antiseptic, an atmosphere of mutual confidence, mutual helpfulness, composure, cheerfulness and humour. How cleansing and salutary it is if in the press and turmoil where each one has to bear his share of inconvenience, there is to be found someone who manages to preserve his composure, and with a touch of dry humour can make a witty remark. Now, such an atmosphere of trust and helpfulness and humour springs from the spirit of patience.
<|det|>text [122, 369, 951, 866]<|/det|>Again, impatience is mischievous in regard to our relations with our conquerors. There is no doubt about it that their hand lies heavy upon us. But it will only become lighter when we win their confidence. In fact in proportion as we have gained it, we have noticed their readiness to meet us halfway and their good will in endeavouring to ease our lot. By impatience we can only aggravate our evil situation in the world. How cheap are the wild accusations which fly to and fro! How cheap and how foolish! Were we patient we should not always be thinking of what must ever seem so unpleasant and deplorable when foreign troops have occupied our soil. Nor should we be only thinking of the worse things that have been spared us (for things might have been much worse) but we should consider above all the positive help that has been given us, and that readiness to meet us which has been manifested again and again. The spirit of patience affords us calm of mind for reflection and so the ability to cleave to the good rather than become obsessed by the evil. Impatience forgets the misery that we ourselves have brought upon the world and the suffering we have caused to so many peoples. Will they so easily forget these miseries? We need patience in order to regain their confidence.
