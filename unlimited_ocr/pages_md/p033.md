<|det|>text [58, 153, 877, 558]<|/det|>This, to be sure, is how a wise administration calculates and we can be thankful for this development. But as Christians we must say still more. As Christians we believe that we have not deeply enough understood the meaning of our joy in nature which discloses its beauty to us in the spring if we regard this joy in biological terms, as it were, as something to be cultivated for the sake of the health of our nation. As Christians we must go still further. In what way? What is the real gift of nature which the generous hand of spring lavishes upon us as a shower of blossoms? What is the real meaning of the joy which awakens within us and fills our being? There can be no doubt as to the answer. The real gift of nature is that it speaks to us of God the Creator; and the real meaning of our joy is that here we feel ourselves coming into communion with the very ground of our being and perceiving its sustaining energies. Thus our joy passes into thankfulness and adoration, adoration of the presence of God in nature.
