<|det|>text [65, 130, 868, 628]<|/det|>Lord, abide with us, for the day is far spent and evening has come. To-night we have gathered in Your presence, because the work of the term which in past weeks has pressed upon us is now coming to an end. Lord, we have indeed tried to carry out our tasks as in Your service. But You alone know whether we have done them well or ill; You know too how often our faithfulness turned into unfaithfulness, how often our good will failed to become effective, and also how often we were lacking in good will. In this evening hour of worship, allow us to place the past weeks in the light and splendour of Your grace, so that the work we have done may, in spite of everything, turn into blessing for us and bear fruit for the future. Our work has bound us together into a fellowship of love, in which you have allowed us again and again to feel the healing touch of Your grace. For many of us it is a fellowship which, from an external point of view, will last but a short time. Grant that for us all it may remain an inner communion of the spirit, and may become ever more and more a linking of heart and mind in devotion to the cause which we serve, and which is the cause of Your kingdom.
<|det|>text [65, 628, 868, 788]<|/det|>Lord God, who once commanded the light to shine forth out of darkness, grant that a bright light may also spring up in our hearts, and that through us the light of the knowledge of the glory of God in the face of Jesus Christ may increasingly illuminate the minds of men. O Lord, abide with us, for the day is far spent and the shadows of evening fall around us! Amen.
