<|det|>text [132, 36, 955, 378]<|/det|>"The Lord is my portion"; that means, I confess my faith in the Lord, in my God. He is my portion, which is to say: if I am confronted by the choice (and we are all confronted by this choice)—here reigns God in all His inscrutability and terror, God in the majesty of His sovereign will, with His inexorable command, and irrevocable decrees—there, the sway of the world with all that it can give, the possibilities which entice my desire and set a goal to my strivings; if I am confronted by this choice, then "the Lord is my portion", it is the Lord whom I choose. I choose the Lord although then I am building only upon faith and hope and have nothing visible and tangible in my hands; although then I can foresee and calculate nothing, and, humanly speaking, stand wrapped in complete darkness; although then, with all my plans and activities, I must ever make the reservation:
