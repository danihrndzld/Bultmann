<|det|>text [79, 239, 897, 485]<|/det|>For the real miracle is just this—that the good news of Christ is preached to the poor. The poor; that is to say, those who suffer in this visible world and who wait for the world to come. And what is the message preached to them? It may be summed up in one word: freedom from the present world. The Gospel has the power to free us from the oppression of the world, because it is the message of God's grace and the forgiveness of sins. It tells us that we become free from the world and from ourselves, when we honestly and unreservedly commit ourselves to God who assures us of His grace, in the word and the person of Jesus Christ.
<|det|>text [79, 486, 897, 893]<|/det|>The Word of God's grace makes all things new for the believer. He who is to come has in truth come and has renewed the world, for those who allow their being to be renewed and transformed by Him. "Therefore if any one is in Christ, he is a new creation" (2 Cor. 5: 17). Now there exists the possibility of the total renewal of humanity, and to one whose being is thus renewed, the world too is renewed. "The light of eternal life shines here and gives the world a new appearance." In what way has the world become new? Paul describes this transformation of the world when he paints the picture of the true servants of God: they live their life and fulfil their ministry "through great endurance, in afflictions, hardships, calamities, beatings, imprisonments, tumults . . .; by purity, knowledge, forbearance, kindness, the Holy Spirit, genuine love, truthful speech and the power of God; with the weapons of righteousness for the right hand and for the left; in honour and dishonour, in ill repute and good repute.
