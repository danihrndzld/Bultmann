And this basic sin of denying God as Creator and asserting one's own right to life is working out its terrible consequences in every department of life; it is like a poison which is ruining both the life of the individual and of the community. From the fact that men assert and insist on their right, that they first and foremost make their claims to life instead of asking what is required of them, there arises envy and hatred, contempt for the laws of nature which sustain life, strife and distress both on a small and a big scale.
