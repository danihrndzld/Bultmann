<|det|>text [121, 207, 946, 431]<|/det|>To-day there is a quite special reason to enlarge on the theme that the gospel signifies a confirmation of the world of the spirit, that grace does not cancel the claim of the ideal but brings out its validity, and at the same time bestows the power to live in obedience to this claim in inner harmony of life. The reason is that in recent years we have learnt what an inner, and then later, outer catastrophe befalls a people when it loses its faith in the ideal world, and rejects the duty of obedience to that world; when in fact the ideal and the spiritual are replaced by false doctrines.
<|det|>text [121, 433, 946, 906]<|/det|>For this is the meaning of what has happened to our nation! We have been told that it is not right but power which establishes order in the life of a people and of the world; that the sole meaning of right is what is expedient for the nation. The ideal of humanity has been despised; the moral law and the claims of conscience have been depreciated as the discoveries of a slavish disposition. The only valid obligation, we have heard, arises not from the world of the spirit, but from blood and soil, and is nothing else than the will to self-assertion and power springing from the race. And just as the sphere of the right and of the moral law was destroyed, so was also the sphere of truth. We have been taught that there is no such thing as absolute truth, and that every insight is only a vision of things determined by the unconscious impulse and will of the blood. There should be no impartial search for truth, no investigation undeterred by whatever its findings happen to be; all knowledge is to be harnessed to the service of the will to power.
