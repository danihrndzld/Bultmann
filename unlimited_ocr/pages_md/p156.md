<|det|>text [115, 289, 940, 646]<|/det|>This situation calls our attention to the most important point of all, namely that the creative action of God is not something which lies unbarred to the eyes of all so that all must see and recognize it. No, God's creative action can only be apprehended when I experience something of it in my own life. Belief in God the Creator of heaven and earth is not a theoretical truth the rightness of which may be realized on the ground of demonstrative reasons, and of which a man might persuade himself once for all so as to be able to say: "If God is the Creator of the world, then He is also my Creator; if He is the Author of all that is, then He is also the Author of my own destiny." Belief of this kind often collapses sharply when a man is struck down by a dark and tragic fate. If he is no longer convinced that God is his Creator, he also ceases to be convinced that God must be the Creator of the world.
<|det|>text [115, 649, 940, 898]<|/det|>The truth in fact is that I can only believe in God as the Creator of the world, when I see that truth reflected in my own life and experience.
