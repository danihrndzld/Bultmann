<|det|>text [113, 630, 938, 903]<|/det|>My text is taken from the farewell discourses of St. John's Gospel. The scene is the last night of Jesus with His disciples; the solemn meal has been partaken of; the way to Geth-semane is now imminent. Thus the context is a scene from the passion narrative. Nevertheless, the Church has not seen fit to order the reading of this passage on any of the passion Sundays, but on this Sunday, the third Sunday before Whitsunday as in fact it has distributed the whole of this chapter of St. John among the Sundays before Whitsunday, for the reason that the words of Jesus here reported are intended to prepare the disciples for the pentecostal gift of the Holy Spirit.
