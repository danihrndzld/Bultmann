But that too would be a peace such as the world gives, since it would be we ourselves who fight to conquer it. The peace which the word of Jesus promises is the peace of eternity, which abides with us at the very heart of our inner disquiet and the storms which beat upon us, just because it is not our peace but God's peace, which transcends our tumult and unrest. By this peace we gain the power to face our inner distresses, so that we do not attempt to impose silence on them, but rather let them speak out loudly. Thus, so far as is possible, we are enabled to comfort sorrow and to soothe pain, and we who know something of the peace of eternity are thus strengthened to deliver the message of this peace to the world, and to convey its very spirit to the world.
<|det|>text [65, 381, 885, 516]<|/det|>“Peace I leave with you. My peace I give to you. Not as the world gives, give I to you. Let not your hearts be troubled, neither let them be afraid.” This is the voice of the love of God who calls us to Himself, and calls us to come to Him as He invites us to come to this celebration of the Lord’s Supper.
