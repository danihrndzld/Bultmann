In the course of his sermon my father had said: "If the farmers were to say in present circumstances—'Why should we give thanks, seeing that after all our labour we have gathered in hardly anything?' then the answer must be: 'Give thanks for the fact that you were able to work!'"
<|det|>text [117, 219, 940, 555]<|/det|>That is certainly a very simple thought, but the fact that just this thought had remained so firmly in the old man's memory shows how a quite simple thought can often strike people with surprise and shed light on their situation. "Give thanks that you were able to work!" How many a sick person would count himself fortunate if only he could work! And to-day above all it should be clear to us when we recall that so many of our friends who have been torn from their work would give much if only they were allowed to carry on with their normal job at home. Which of us can really doubt that the very fact of our being allowed to work is in itself a proof of God's goodness to us? And seeing that we are permitted to work, are we going to lament and complain that there are other people who are better off than ourselves? Often we do not realize how well off we in fact are!
<|det|>text [117, 557, 940, 895]<|/det|>I believe that to-day especially we need to take the lesson of our parable to heart. To-day a new social order is taking shape in our midst; and we are asked to be serious about it so that all inequalities in our social life, all class warfare, may disappear and a new community of the people based on social equality and equality of opportunity may be successfully established. Has our parable anything to say in opposition to this idea? Nothing at all! We have in fact already suggested that our text is not concerned to impart teaching about the social order. On the contrary it is quite right that in our human communal life we should be careful to achieve an order of society that is based on justice, and in which there will be given to every man who is capable of work and glad to work equal opportunities of prosperity and advancement.
