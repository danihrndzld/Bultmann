<|det|>text [108, 516, 938, 901]<|/det|>THE season of Advent proclaims the coming of Christ to judge the world, and on the second Sunday in Advent, according to ancient Church use, we are to think of the theme of divine judgment. The solemn summons of the text: “Behold, I stand at the door and knock”, also invites us to such thoughts. It admonishes us to be prepared for the coming of the Lord, and the preceding verses suggest how we should acquire this spirit of vigilant readiness. They are introduced by the remarkable word: “The words of the Amen, the faithful and true witness, the beginning of God’s creation.” This means that the words with which we are challenged, John the seer has heard in the spirit as the words of Christ, the words of the “Amen”. Amen signifies validity: what is unshakably true. Hence we are to hear these words as the solid abidingly true message of Him in whom God Himself became for us the incarnate Word, a living and true summons.
