This is only apparently true, for in reality every man, whether he cares to admit it or not, is in the last resort concerned about his standing before God. For why does he wish to be recognized and distinguished by other men? At bottom not because he attaches so much importance to other men and their opinions; he may despise them even while hankering after their recognition. At bottom he needs their appreciation because he wishes to be certain of himself. In the last analysis every man feels that his existence is of questionable and dubious value; there lurks in the mind of every man the fear of nothingness and the void, and the fear that it may also be true of him to say: "Weighed in the balances and found wanting." And the life of men consists largely in the effort to delude oneself about these inner realities, and to suppress these fears. Man tries to do this by various means, and it is this very thing which he is trying to do in his search for recognition and prestige. Being aware of the emptiness of his inner life, he clings to the picture that others form of him. In this picture he as it were sees himself mirrored, he holds fast to it, and views in it the reflection of his true self. And just for this reason the picture must be as great and imposing as possible. In essence it is not in the eyes of others that he wishes to stand out as great, but in his own eyes; in essence he wishes to enjoy credit not before others but before himself.
<|det|>text [50, 621, 885, 894]<|/det|>Before himself! And that in the last resort means before God. It is God who faces him with this pressing question, whether he has something to show or whether he has lived in vain and worthlessly. He feels that he is responsible for his being and that he is called to make something of it. All human longing for recognition is ultimately aimed at providing an answer to that decisive question: am I, generally speaking, a creature of worth or am I worthless? Now of course it can make a great deal of difference whether and to what an extent a man reckons with God in his life or not.
