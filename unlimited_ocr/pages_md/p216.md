But everyone can live the life of the spirit who remains faithful to the good and the true; and this we can do whatever the distress and hardship of the present hour. Anyone may experience the strengthening power of the spirit who fulfils his duties with such loyalty. It must not be the self-seeking urge to snatch as much as possible for oneself, to save one's own skin at all costs, which determines the course of our daily life, but rather the sway of honesty and kindness in our hearts even in the face of cruel hardship. How much easier could men make their own lot if they met each other in the spirit of friendliness and good will, were prepared to allow to the other fellow his due, and to bear with patience the misunderstandings which are unavoidable! How unnecessarily hard men make life for themselves by their impatience and bitterness, their unfriendliness and lovelessness! But whoever remains faithful to the life of the spirit will always find such as share his faith. Such faith is the basis of true fellowship; and fellowship in the life of the spirit sustains and strengthens by its inner bonds, and is also effectively manifested in outward helpfulness.
