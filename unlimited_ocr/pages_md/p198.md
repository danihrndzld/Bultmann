<|det|>text [111, 220, 934, 401]<|/det|>“Blessed are the poor in spirit.” What does it mean to be poor in spirit? It means to realize one’s poverty in the presence of God. No doubt in virtue of our essential nature as men we are all poor in the sight of God, helpless and frail, weak and perishable mortals. The point is, however—do we realize this fact? Do we realize it, not in the sense of theoretical knowledge, but in such a way that this awareness penetrates our whole being and informs our whole way of life?
<|det|>text [111, 402, 937, 899]<|/det|>To be poor in spirit means not to conceal the essential poverty of our human nature by the apparent wealth which a man may acquire. It may be a question of outward prosperity; as Luther says, to be poor in spirit means “a heart which does not cleave to property and wealth, but, even when a man has it, it is for him as though he had it not”.
