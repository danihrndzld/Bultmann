<|det|>text [73, 225, 894, 470]<|/det|>Someone, however, may be asking the question: “Yes, but for what exactly should we be prepared? When did the second invitation come to us: ‘Come, for all is now ready’—has it not been lacking?” This question leads us to remember that whenever a nation as a whole turns away from the Church, it is always individuals who are guilty of the defection. It is the individual to whom the second invitation is issued, the call: “Come, for all is now ready.” And it comes in fact just when he is not expecting it, when it is least convenient, with the consequence that he prefers to close his ears to it.
