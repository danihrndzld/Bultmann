<|det|>text [67, 582, 892, 763]<|/det|>Our parable is easily intelligible in itself. It illustrated the relation existing between a master and his slave (for the "servant" of the parable is a slave). The slave is the property of his master, and stands wholly at the disposal of the latter. The master has unlimited right to his service. The slave has no rights at all and is not in a position to raise any claims. All that he does he is obliged to do, and he has no reason to expect any thanks from his master.
