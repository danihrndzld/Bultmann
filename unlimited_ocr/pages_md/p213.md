<|det|>text [62, 118, 885, 682]<|/det|>I do not now say all this in order to launch a cheap accusation against a system which has collapsed, but in order to warn all men to open their eyes and see on what a precipitous slope modern life in general has been moving. The false doctrines of the “national-socialist” system are only an extreme radical consequence of that development which has been taking place in recent decades, in fact during the whole of the past century. The belief in spiritual values has faded more and more. A nationalistic will to power has increasingly controlled political thought. Considerations of utility, of practical advancement and profit, have increasingly moulded education, the choice of a profession and the conduct of life in general. The question of what a young man should learn has been more and more considered from the point of view: can it be of use to him later in life? That has been asked rather than such primary questions as: Will it develop his mind? Will it give to his life a content independent of practical success, a content which will guard him against temptation and raise him above the vexations and disappointments of life? How often has there been talk of a knowledge which is remote from the practical concerns of life! It easily occurs to everyone that knowledge deviates from life and chases vain fantasies. But to whom does it occur that life itself may often in one way or another stray from the truth which in the realm of knowledge is honoured as guide?
