<|det|>text [108, 84, 934, 333]<|/det|>“Behold, I stand at the door and knock.” Once more in the season of Advent the summoning message rings out, admonishing us to look with concentrated gaze on Him who is the very image of divine love, and who is also in consequence—and the second Sunday in Advent teaches us to think of this—the Judge of the whole world. For so it is: the depths of divine love are opened out only to him who allows himself to be emancipated from his attachment to the things of time and space and who allows Christ to effect this redemptive work of emancipation in his life. May we prepare and dispose our hearts for this work of divine redemption!
