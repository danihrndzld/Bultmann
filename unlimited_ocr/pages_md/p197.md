<|det|>text [60, 444, 888, 669]<|/det|>THE essential meaning of the beatitudes is their implication of a promise. For this reason they form a most suitable text for the season of Advent; for the Advent message is the message of a promise. A promise for those who are oppressed by adversity and cry aloud for help; for those who “walk in darkness” and long for light. To all such the promise rings out: “Blessed are ye!” “Salvation will be yours!” For to them the future is to bring help and healing; they are to be comforted and filled; they are to see God; the kingdom of heaven is theirs.
