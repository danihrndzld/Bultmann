<|det|>text [82, 272, 907, 700]<|/det|>Let us then receive in patience, as from God's hand, what He sends us, and address our hopes to the world of unseen realities. It is just in so doing that we shall win true inner freedom for the urgent tasks of the present, courage to accomplish the work which in the distress of our days is laid upon us. For then no disillusionment can paralyse our strength; then we have become unassailable. Patience which, abandoning earthly hopes, nevertheless continues to hope, bears within itself the seeds of fulfilment. The way into the darkness of death is already lightened by the dawn of resurrection life. If we decide for God, we shall become inwardly aware that God has already decided for us. Then we shall not only “rejoice in our hope of sharing the glory of God”, but, “more than that, we rejoice in our sufferings, knowing that suffering produces endurance, and endurance produces character, and character produces hope, and hope does not disappoint us, because God’s love has been poured into our hearts through the Holy Spirit which has been given to us” (Rom. 5: 3-5).
