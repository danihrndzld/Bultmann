<|det|>text [102, 438, 933, 802]<|/det|>For the Third Sunday in Advent the Church chooses the gospel concerning the question of John the Baptist, so that from it we may learn the right way to prepare ourselves. This gospel tells us how the Baptist, cast into prison by Herod Antipas, sent two of his disciples to Jesus with the question: "Are you he who is to come?" Many readers are puzzled to know how the Baptist came to ask Jesus such a question. Did he not already know Jesus? Must it be that doubts arose in his mind with regard to Jesus? Or was it that the disciples were to ask Jesus this question not to satisfy any curiosity on the part of the Baptist, but for their own good, that they might hear from the lips of Jesus Himself that He was the One who should come? We do not know and ought not to seek to know. The story is told us only in order that we might hear this decisive question and might make it our own: "Are you he who is to come?"
