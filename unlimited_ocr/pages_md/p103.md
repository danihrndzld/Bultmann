And the final phrase expresses the full seriousness of the decision: “Blessed is he who takes no offence at me.” It is therefore clear that if Jesus is the One who is to come, He is not so in the plain unequivocal way which longings and hopes, desires and fancies picture. He is not so in the sense which men imagine that He must be. That He should be the One who is to come is an astonishing fact, and for many repulsive and offensive. As to whether He truly is the One who is to come, the burden of decision rests on us.

For us, these miracles do not constitute proof! No, and neither were they a proof at the time of their taking place; they did not convince everyone and in fact were rather for many a cause of offence.
