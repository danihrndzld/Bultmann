This means, however, when man is torn away into the heart of darkness, and does not know what he should love, when he only knows what he should not love; when he repudiates all that he can know and experience, and longs only for the One whom he does not yet know."6 "Thus hope bears us away into the unknown, the hidden, the innermost darkness. It knows not for what it should hope; it knows only for what it must not hope."7
