<|det|>text [106, 204, 926, 653]<|/det|>We have not spoken specifically about the fate which at the present time has overtaken us, and the terrifying and tragic nature, of which we are painfully conscious, of this very day. Is it necessary to speak of it specially now? Whoever has understood all that is implied in the demands of Christian vigilance and preparedness, about which our parable warns us, will also realize what these demands imply just now above all. No doubt even without meditation on this parable we shall all know that we must hold ourselves ready to meet whatever the future may bring by our readiness for action and self-sacrifice, and by calm bravery. Beyond all this, however, our parable warns us to be prepared for what God wishes to say to us in the events which loom ahead. It teaches us to seek the firm ground on which alone we can find genuine peace and the inspiration for courage, on which we can win an inner detachment from everything that our earthly future may bring of good and ill, victories and sacrifices—that inner detachment which we win when we are ready for God's future, and cling to Him in calm trust.
