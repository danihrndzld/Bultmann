But has such pride, such self-security, any longer its convincing power in these days when our world, in which we dwelt with a sense of pride and security, is falling into ruins before our eyes? Does not such an attitude now stand out as the arrogance of man who forgets that he is poor, that he is futile?
<|det|>text [79, 545, 900, 905]<|/det|>“Blessed are the poor in spirit”—the word is intended to comfort the poor who realize their poverty. But it does not comfort them by reinterpreting their poverty as riches; it does not mean: in the awareness of your poverty resides your wealth! It is true that of all human attitudes such irony and humour stand nearest to the Christian faith.
