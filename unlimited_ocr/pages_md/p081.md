<|det|>text [55, 46, 879, 589]<|/det|>Work however not only makes the body tired, and is physically fatiguing, but it can also consume our inner strength. Those who are engaged in work which with its plans and prospects claims their whole attention, are always in danger of forgetting their souls through their absorption in such work. For such people the time for recollection, and self-examination in the presence of God, for realizing the primordial necessity of God's blessing on their work, becomes ever shorter and in the end threatens to vanish altogether. We say of course that "so and so is utterly taken up with his work" and more often than not we do not realize what a condemnation this implies. A man of this kind must lose the whole basis and poise of his life if he has the misfortune to meet with failure, for it was only his work which gave his life its support; and if such a one becomes incapable of work, then he loses the content of his life completely, for it was only his work which gave his life its content and meaning. Thus it appears that work can be not only a blessing but also a curse, and can, as it were, eat up and dissipate a man's life. We detect in the people of our acquaintance whether they are merely workers (rather like working animals) as opposed to human beings, for such people are incapable of taking part in a genuine community life, and sharing the joys and sorrows of others.
