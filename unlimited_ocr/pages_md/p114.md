<|det|>text [106, 228, 934, 408]<|/det|>Yes, we often wish for each other light in the darkness of our days and in the mystery of our destiny. But we shall only receive it when we pray for light to illuminate the darkness of our inner being. Discipleship implies the confession that here in our inmost being the darkness is thickest, it implies the readiness to receive our true selves as a gift from the hands of God, and to allow ourselves to be re-created as children of light in the light which streams from the Holy Child.
