<|det|>text [48, 86, 870, 265]<|/det|>By this means our life gains clarity and peace. This does not mean that in the ground plan of our life, in its purposes and aims, we embody an intelligent solution to the riddle of existence. Every question which is aimed at mastering the secret pattern of existence must in fact be silenced; and we remain ignorant of the goal to which God is leading us. No, our life gains clarity only because everything He sends us we may and must receive as the gift of His love:
<|det|>text [50, 267, 870, 333]<|/det|>"Let His loving glance deeply penetrate your soul, and His eternal light and joy will flood your being. Heart and mind and spirit shall then awaken to new life."
<|det|>text [48, 335, 874, 786]<|/det|>Luminous transparency must also penetrate our life in so far as it is a life of responsibility and action. We do not suggest that the Christian should be able to organize his life in accordance with a completely clear plan. The clarity which the divine light gives is not the clarity of a programme, but the clarity which flows from the awareness of being sustained by the divine love which frees us for the manifestation of love in our own lives. Our eyes are then opened to the truth that we ourselves can and must be bearers of divine light and love in the world; that the decisions with which each day and moment face us should be made in the spirit of love; that the plans and the projects which our responsibilities in daily life constantly demand from us, should be conceived in the spirit of love. We are able to see how much others need us and where and how we can help them. Thus our life becomes filled with a new radiance and transparency; thus our true self finds its fulfilment, if we no longer will to live for ourselves and by our own resources, but in the light of the love of God and in the service of others.
<|det|>text [50, 786, 875, 900]<|/det|>“Divinity, of which man has so often been afraid, has now been imparted to us. In the south and the north, it has awakened to life seeds of eternity. Let us then, in the richly dowered garden of God, faithfully await the blooming of each bud and blossom.”
