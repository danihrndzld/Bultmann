<|det|>text [58, 40, 885, 452]<|/det|>Not so long ago humanity was proud of its culture and progress. The world war was required to make it realize, and in the most cruel way, what dark passions still slumber, ineffaceably present, in the depths of the human heart. Has the terror which this discovery aroused in us so quickly faded from our minds? Again we hear praise of what will-power and energy have accomplished in recent years, and proud words in view of the tasks which are still to be done. This is all very fine. But is modern man no longer threatened by the dark powers of evil which lurk in the secret recesses of his own heart? And has he not cause to think of them precisely in view of the immense tasks which it is incumbent on him now to achieve? And must we not recoil in dismay when in the boastings of the arrogant it seems that the awareness of the mystery of evil has been completely forgotten? And would it not appear that in such words the fear of evil has been forcibly suppressed? Is it not as if, in the pride which refuses to hear sin spoken of, this very fear was secretly lurking?
<|det|>text [58, 453, 885, 885]<|/det|>When we are told of outbreaks of evil in distant parts, no doubt we feel distressed and horrified. And we can understand how the reader of books which give an account of the cruelties of the communist reign of terror or of that unforgettable little book by Dwinger, Zug durch Sibirien (Journey through Siberia) feels gripped by pity for the victims of these cruelties of which it is hard to believe that men have been able to perpetrate them on their fellow men. It is also understandable that in face of such horrors people ask: "How could God allow it?—if in fact there is a God at all!" Has every reader allowed these things to strike home to his conscience also? Or do we take it so much for granted that we ourselves could never be guilty of these atrocities, that the thought or question never occurs to us whether every man who participates in evil is not in part responsible for what the massed forces of evil are doing on this earth? When we have heard of these dark deeds, have we only called God to account, have we not realized that we ourselves were being called to account?
