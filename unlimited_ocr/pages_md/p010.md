Straining forward eagerly,
In lonely wrestling, to Thee I lift my hands,
To Thee I flee.
Thou, to whom in deepest faith,
In solemn awe, I have hallowed altars,
That Thy voice may ever echo in my soul.
"Thereon, deep engraved in letters of flame,
Stands written: to the unknown God.
His I am, e'en though in wanton shame
I tarried to the present hour:
His I am and I feel the snares
Which grip and twine and hold me low,
And—though fain I would flee—
Compel me to His service.
"Thou unknown God, Thee will I know,
Thou who piercest my heart,
And in stormy anger dost sweep my soul.
Inapprehensible, yet to me akin!
Thee will I know, Thyself will I serve."
