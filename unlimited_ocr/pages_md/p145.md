The legend relates how God once walked the earth as a simple wayfarer, and sought a shelter for the night. He knocked at the door of a rich man and asked for sleeping accommodation. The rich man looked at the unpromising wayfarer who stood at his door, and whose appearance did not exactly suggest that He would be able to pay well, and on various pretexts he refused Him shelter. It was just not convenient. God then knocked at the door of the poor man's house, where He found a friendly reception. As the tale goes on to say, the rich man had punished himself, whereas the poor man was granted a rich blessing.
